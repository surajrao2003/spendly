Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application (the "Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly when creating an account and using the Service, such as your name, email address, and password. We also collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions, so that Spendly can display your spending history and summaries back to you.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve the Service — for example, to authenticate your account, display your expenses and spending patterns, and generate category breakdowns and summaries. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party services for functionality such as font hosting or infrastructure. These providers only receive the minimum information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page. We will respond to reasonable requests regarding your personal data as soon as possible.
